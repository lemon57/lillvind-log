We met on the boat around 7:00 AM and motored out of the harbor between 7:30 and 7:40. Once clear, we hoisted the jib and mainsail. There wasn't much wind, so we were making about 3.5 knots. We sailed until we reached a depth of 17 meters opposite Västra Hamnen. There, we hove to and tried drift fishing for cod, but we didn't get a single bite. We did discover, however, that the best way to slow our drift was to put the rudder hard over to one side, depending on which side was leeward. We repositioned and drifted a few more times, but still caught nothing. Not even a nibble.

At 11:00 AM, we set the sails again and headed back toward our home berth. The wind had picked up, and with a full mainsail and jib, we flew back incredibly fast, hitting speeds of 5.5 to 6.5 knots. We decided to use the opportunity to practice entering the harbor strictly under sail.

We dropped the jib but kept the main up. Roman took the rudder while Ivan handled the sail trim. Initially, we started the engine and left it idling just in case, but we soon shut it off to make it a true test. Both the wind speed and direction were absolutely perfect for practicing this maneuver. We reached our slip a bit slower than usual, but we did it entirely under sail — a first for us on this boat.

All in all, we figured out how to use our fishing rods, got in some good sailing practice, and successfully maneuvered into the harbor under sail. We may not have caught any fish, but we brought home plenty of valuable experience.

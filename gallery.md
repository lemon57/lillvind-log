A collection of favourite shots from aboard Lillvind.

---

![Sunset behind the Öresund Bridge, seen from the water off Malmö]({{ '/assets/images/behind_the_oresund_bridge_2025.jpg' | relative_url }})

**Behind the bridge, Malmö, 2025.** Sailing out past the Öresund Bridge and catching the sun as it dropped right between the pylons. A golden track on the water, and the kind of sky that makes you stop trimming the sails for a moment and just look.

---

![Øresund Bridge from behind, heading toward Peberholm island]({{ '/assets/images/trip-swe-kph-bridge-to-peberholm-2026-05-01.jpg' | relative_url }})

**Behind the bridge, heading to Peberholm, May 2026.** Just past the bridge heading south, Peberholm island off to starboard. Shallow water forces you wide here — the bridge looms behind you and the open Øresund opens up ahead.

---

![Timber cargo ship on the shipping lane, Øresund]({{ '/assets/images/trip-swe-kph-timber-ship-2026-05-01.jpg' | relative_url }})

**Timber carrier on the shipping lane, May 2026.** One of the big ships we had to cross ahead of — stacked with timber, making good speed through the lane. You pick your gap and go.

---

![Wind farm off Sundby harbour, Copenhagen]({{ '/assets/images/trip-swe-kph-windfarm-sundby-2026-05-01.jpg' | relative_url }})

**Wind farm, Sundby approach, May 2026.** The smaller cluster of turbines that sits right in front of Sundby harbour. When you can see these clearly you know you're almost in.

---

![Øresund Bridge with a bird and open sky]({{ '/assets/images/trip-swe-kph-bridge-bird-sky-2026-05-01.jpg' | relative_url }})

**Bird and bridge, May 2026.** The bridge against open sky — a bird hanging in the frame just long enough to make the shot. Some photos just happen.

---

![Øresund Bridge, calm sea, sun on the water]({{ '/assets/images/trip-swe-kph-bridge-calm-sea-2026-05-01.jpg' | relative_url }})

**Bridge and calm water, May 2026.** Flat calm all the way. The sun laid a track across the water straight toward the bridge. A good day to be out.
